# Draft buyer outreach

Work from qualified candidates, the user's real offer and approved claims. Load [qualification gates](../qualify-buyer-intent/references/qualification.md) if needed. Prefer the platform's intended reply or application route. Do not enrich private contact details or turn public posts into a bulk contact database.

For each requested draft:

1. Confirm there is a current, relevant buying request, a valid source URL, a supported reply route and no suppression or explicit no-contact instruction. Watch-list items, seller posts, diagnostics and uncertain publication dates need evidence review before outreach. If source rules prohibit promotional replies, recommend a permitted action instead.
2. Read the source quote and separate it from inference. Refer to the exact requested outcome naturally; do not imply a relationship, access, experience, certification, savings, price or outcome the user has not supplied.
3. Write one helpful opening, a specific relevant suggestion or offer, and one low-pressure question. Keep social replies around 40–90 words and proposals around 100–180 words unless requested otherwise. For an anonymous job client, use a neutral greeting and refer to the project, without guessing a name/company.
4. Return the draft with the source link, why it fits, any unresolved claim and the intended platform. Follow the user's tone. Do not mention an internal lead score to the prospect.

Generating a draft does not authorize sending it. Do not post, DM, email, apply to a job, or mutate a CRM. This skills-only plugin supplies no sending integration; if the user later authorizes a specific external action, use only the host's available, appropriate capability and verify the result. Avoid repeated unsolicited messaging and never evade opt-outs or platform restrictions.
