# Privacy

Effective date: October 11, 2026.

Buyer Intent Lead Finder is a collection of skills and reference files published by Khadin Akbar. It has no plugin-operated backend, telemetry collector, contact database or account system.

## Local work

Claude can analyze exports, qualify candidates, create reports and draft replies using files you supply. Keep those files and operation receipts in a private working folder outside the plugin. Their retention follows your choices and your host application's settings. The plugin does not upload these files to the publisher. The conversation and files you give Claude are handled under your Claude account's policies and settings.

## Live Apify searches

When you authorize a live search, the official Apify CLI sends selected query terms, search scope, count limits and Actor inputs to Apify. The selected Actor may use its configured data provider or source access route to retrieve public posts or contract projects. Query terms can therefore be processed by those services. Use focused commercial queries and avoid including sensitive personal or customer information.

Apify stores run metadata, input records, datasets and available output/summary records according to its platform controls and your account settings. The plugin publisher also publishes the selected Actors; this plugin does not promise that Apify or an Actor owner has no access to cloud run information. Consult [Apify's privacy policy](https://apify.com/privacy-policy), Actor documentation and your account settings for their processing and retention.

The CLI uses your existing local login to communicate with Apify. The skills do not read credential files, print tokens or request source cookies. Do not include credentials in queries, files shared for support or public reports.

## Results and outreach

Results may include public usernames, public profile links, post text, publication dates and project metadata. The workflows minimize reports to relevant commercial evidence, source links and known facts. They do not infer private emails, reveal anonymous client identities or merge people across sources without corroboration. Replies remain drafts unless you separately authorize a specific external action through an available host capability.

Deleting local exports or Apify cloud records is your choice; plugin installation does not automatically delete service records. The plugin creates no recurring task or delivery destination. Do not post private leads or account details in [support issues](https://github.com/khadinakbarlabs/buyer-intent-lead-finder-claude/issues).
